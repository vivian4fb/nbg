The 4 AM problem

Low oxygen is the silent killer in every pond.

Every night, while you sleep, dissolved oxygen in your pond crashes. Fish stop eating, stop growing — and on a bad night, you lose the whole crop.

🌙

Night-time DO crash

After sunset, algae stop making oxygen and start consuming it. By 4 AM, DO can fall below 2 mg/L — the danger zone for every farmed species.

இரவில் ஆக்ஸிஜன் வீழ்ச்சி
🐌

Stunted growth, wasted feed

Fish under oxygen stress digest poorly. Feed conversion worsens, growth slows, and your biggest cost — feed — goes to waste.

தீவனம் வீண், வளர்ச்சி மந்தம்
⚠️

One bad night = total loss

A single severe DO crash can wipe out months of investment in hours. Paddle wheels churn the surface, but oxygen escapes as fast as it enters.

The science, simply

Bubbles so small,
they never leave the water.

A paddle wheel splashes oxygen in; physics takes it right back out. Oxyniti shears air into bubbles thousands of times thinner than a human hair. At that size they stop rising, stay suspended, and transfer oxygen with near-total efficiency — day and night.

1

Draw & shear

The unit draws pond water and injects air through a nano-shear chamber, generating trillions of <200 nm bubbles.

2

Infuse the whole pond

The milky nano-bubble plume spreads through the water column — not just the surface — raising DO everywhere fish live.

3

Hold it for days

Neutral buoyancy and negative surface charge keep bubbles suspended, buffering your pond through the dangerous night hours.
